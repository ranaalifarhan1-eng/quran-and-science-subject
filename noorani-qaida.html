Learning Progression

Why Noorani Qaida is the most reliable first step.

The Noorani Qaida method breaks down Arabic reading into manageable building blocks. Students avoid guessing and learn the exact mechanics of letter pronunciation, vocal movements, and connecting sounds.

33 Structured Lessons Makharij Correction One-to-One Tutor Kids & Adults

Key Learning Milestones

1
Single Letters (Huruf Mufradat): Learn the 29 individual Arabic letters with authentic articulation points.
2
Compound Forms (Murakkabat): Recognize letters when combined at the beginning, middle, and end of words.
3
Short Vowels (Harakaat) & Tanween: Master Zabar, Zer, Pesh, and double-vowel markings without stretching.
4
Sukoon, Tashdeed & Leen: Practice stopping letters, doubled letters, and soft vowel sounds.
5
Foundational Tajweed: Rules of Noon Sakin, Meem Sakin, Madd, Qalqalah, and Quranic stopping signs (Waqf).

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about Noorani Qaida

How long does it typically take to complete Noorani Qaida?

Most students complete the 33 lessons in 3 to 6 months depending on student age, weekly class frequency (2, 3, or 5 days per week), and home practice.

Is this course suitable for complete beginners and children?

Yes. Noorani Qaida is specifically designed for complete beginners starting from the very first letter (Alif). We teach children as young as 4–5 years old, as well as adult learners.

Can female students and young girls learn with a female tutor?

Yes. We have dedicated, qualified female Quran tutors available for sisters and girls across all time zones.

What equipment is needed for online Noorani Qaida classes?

All you need is a computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone with an internet connection and headphones. Our tutors share the digital Qaida lessons directly on screen.
