Common Inquiries

Quick answers before you submit.

Can I choose a male or female tutor?

Yes. The registration form includes tutor preference, subject to the academy's confirmed teacher availability.

Can siblings study at different times?

Yes. Each student can be assigned a separate class time and learning path. Family package rules can be added once the client confirms them.

How are class timings decided?

Students can share preferred timings and time zone. The coordinator can then confirm matching tutor availability.

Can I ask for fee details before the trial?

Yes. Use this same form and mention the course and desired weekly frequency. The academy can share the current monthly fee.
