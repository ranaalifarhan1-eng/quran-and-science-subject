About Quran & Science

Personalized Online Learning, Built Around Every Student.

Our goal is to make structured Quran education and academic tutoring accessible from home, giving every student the attention, flexibility and encouragement they need to grow with confidence.

Book a Free Trial Academic Tutoring
Our Purpose

Education should be personal, clear and accessible for every family.

Quran & Science is being shaped as an online learning academy for students who want dependable one-to-one Quran education and academic tutoring without geographical boundaries or rigid schedules.

The learning model combines live one-to-one teaching, regular revision and level-based progression. In Quran studies, students can begin from Arabic letters and Noorani Qaida, improve reading fluency and Tajweed, work toward Hifz, or study Islamic learning with patient guidance.

In our academic stream, experienced tutors provide focused one-to-one support for O Level, IGCSE, GCSE, A Level, and Pakistan Curriculum (Grade 1–12) with focus on Mathematics and other available subjects. Subject availability is confirmed according to the student's level and requirements.

Learn at your pace

Private classes allow experienced tutors to adapt lesson speed, revision and correction to each individual student.

Who We Teach

Learning paths for different ages and goals.

The academy is designed to support beginners, school-age students, and learners looking to sharpen specific skills.

Young Beginners

Letter recognition, Noorani Qaida, short Surahs, daily duas and foundational Islamic manners.

School-age Students

Quran recitation, Tajweed, Hifz, and academic tutoring for O Level, A Level, and Pakistan curricula.

Adults & Re-learners

Comfortable private lessons for adults beginning again or improving recitation fluency.

Sisters & Girls

Experienced female tutor availability for sisters and girls who prefer female instructors for Quran learning.

Teaching Standards

What we expect from every tutor profile.

Every tutor representing Quran & Science is expected to bring solid subject understanding, clear communication, and a patient, encouraging approach to live one-to-one lessons. We value instructors who understand the nuances of online teaching—adapting their pace for young children taking their first Arabic steps, providing dedicated female instructors for sisters, and offering structured, concept-driven support for students preparing for academic and board exams.
