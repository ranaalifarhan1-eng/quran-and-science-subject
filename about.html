Our Purpose

Quran education should be personal, clear and practical for everyday families.

Quran & Science is being shaped as a virtual academy for students who want dependable online Quran education without the limitations of location or rigid schedules.

The learning model combines live one-to-one teaching, regular correction, revision and level-based progression. Students can begin from Arabic letters and Noorani Qaida, improve reading and Tajweed, work toward Hifz, or study translation and Islamic learning with suitable guidance.

A separate academic stream can support Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry for school students, creating one learning platform for faith-centered and academic development.

Learn at your pace

Private classes allow the tutor to adapt lesson speed, revision and correction.
