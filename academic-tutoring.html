Academic FAQ

Frequently asked questions about academic tutoring.

Which academic subjects are available for tuition?

We offer Mathematics and other available subjects for students up to Grade 12 in Pakistan, as well as students following O Level, IGCSE, GCSE, and A Level curricula. Subject availability is confirmed according to the student's level and requirements during initial consultation.

How are live online tutoring classes conducted?

Classes are conducted live one-to-one using video calling and an interactive digital whiteboard, allowing the tutor and student to write formulas, annotate diagrams, and solve problems collaboratively in real time.

Can we choose class days and timings?

Yes. We offer flexible scheduling to accommodate after-school hours, weekends, and students across different time zones worldwide (UK, US, Canada, Middle East, Pakistan, Australia).

How does the free trial work for academic tutoring?

You can request a free trial to evaluate our teaching method and discuss the student's syllabus with the tutor. There is no upfront obligation before enrolling in a regular plan.

Share the student's curriculum, current grade level, and subject requirements. Our academic coordinator will arrange a trial session with an experienced tutor.
