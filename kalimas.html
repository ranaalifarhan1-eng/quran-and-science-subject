Foundations of Islam

Why Learn the Six Kalimas?

The Six Kalimas summarize the fundamental tenets of Islamic faith, affirming pure monotheism, praising Allah, seeking forgiveness, and rejecting disbelief.

01

Core Islamic Creed (Aqeedah)

Every Kalima reinforces the bedrock pillars of faith—affirming the absolute Oneness of Allah and the prophethood of Muhammad (ﷺ).

02

Accurate Pronunciation

Mastering Arabic letters with proper Tajweed and vocal articulation (Makharij) ensures words are recited correctly without error.

03

Deep Understanding

Learning English and Urdu translations helps students understand the profound spiritual meaning behind every single phrase they recite.

04

Guided Memorization

Patient, one-to-one repetition with an experienced teacher makes memorizing all six Kalimas enjoyable and natural for young learners.

What are the Six Kalimas?

The Six Kalimas are six fundamental Islamic declarations of faith in Arabic: 1. Tayyibah (Purity), 2. Shahadah (Evidence), 3. Tamjeed (Glory), 4. Tawheed (Oneness), 5. Astaghfar (Forgiveness), and 6. Radd-e-Kufr (Rejection of Disbelief). Together they summarize core Islamic Aqeedah.

Can children learn and memorize the Kalimas online?

Yes. Our one-to-one online classes are designed specifically for young learners. Tutors use patient repetition, friendly pacing, and visual aids to help children memorize each Kalima step-by-step.

Do teachers help with Arabic pronunciation and Tajweed?

Absolutely. Every session includes live audio-video correction so students articulate letters from their correct vocal points (Makharij), avoiding pronunciation mistakes early on.

Are English and Urdu translations included in the lessons?

Yes. All learning slides provide authentic Arabic text alongside transliteration, English meanings, and Urdu translations so students comprehend what they recite.

How long does it take to memorize all six Kalimas?

Most students comfortably learn and memorize the six Kalimas within 2 to 4 weeks depending on their age, lesson frequency, and daily revision routine.
